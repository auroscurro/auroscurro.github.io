3D Character Animation

"The Room Where It Happens"

A 30-second 3D animation project synchronized to the Hamilton musical track "The Room Where It Happens". This piece was built under a harsh deadline, completed in approximately 140 hours. The characters belong to Mlle_Physthache, the host of the DTIYS. It took me two months to finish. Before starting I decided I want to make the models as light as I can for animation. I liked the characters and the DND aesthetic and would definetely do it again.

Every step of the production pipeline was handled entirely by me in Blender: 3D modeling, UV unwrapping, texturing, custom character rigging, staging, and keyframed character performance for two characters alongside custom environmental props.

Scene Breakdown & Process:
Working under such a tight time limit meant I had to plan every step carefully. I focused heavily on strong key poses, expressive body language, and making sure every movement felt alive and perfectly synced to the rhythm of the music. Since I handled the entire production pipeline completely by myself, I took care of every single creative and technical stage. I built the characters and all the background props from scratch, making sure the mesh was clean, optimized, and ready for smooth movement. I carefully unwrapped the models and painted the textures to give the scene a stylized, rich, and cohesive look. I set up custom skeletons and controls for the characters, allowing me to easily pose them, control their expressions, and animate their interactions. I spent a lot of time on animation, timing the characters' gestures, glances, and interactions so they would match the rhythm of the song.
