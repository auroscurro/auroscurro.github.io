WHO AM I?

I'm a 3D animator and digital artist specializing in bringing stories and characters to life. Most of the time I'm working in Blender. I have a background in fine art, traditional painting, and ceramics, and that helps me add a strong sense of form, anatomy, and staging into 3D production.

My core focus is full asset production—from modeling and rigging to final render. I am currently available for freelance animation commissions, 3D character work, and studio collaborations!
