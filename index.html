WHO AM I?

I'm an artist who combines skills for digital and traditional art, comic creation, animation, and creating 3D models. It's fun to mix my skills to create solid visual stories.

My work is driven by curiosity and the desire to tell stories that resonate. I am currently available for freelance commissions and collaboration! Whether you need concept art, comic pages, 3D assets, or animated sequences, I offer a full pipeline from sketch to final render.
